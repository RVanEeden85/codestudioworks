# CodeStudioWorks lead-generation plan

Prepared October 1, 2026. Website improvements and consent-based GA4 are published. The next objective is qualified conversations, with a $0 initial advertising budget.

## Focus the initial offer

Lead with custom business websites for local service businesses and professional services. Keep software, apps, SEO and digital marketing available through their dedicated pages, but use one relevant offer in each message.

Suggested positioning:

> Custom websites built directly with a Westland developer. Clear scope, practical enquiry and booking journeys, and ownership after launch. Serving Metro Detroit and remote clients worldwide. Business website projects start from $1,250 USD; the written quote confirms scope and ongoing costs.

Do not reduce the price based only on a small search sample. First establish whether relevant people see the offer, contact the studio and fit the project scope.

## First week

1. **Prove the enquiry path.** Submit a clearly labelled test from a normal browser using an inbox you control. Complete Turnstile yourself. Confirm the success message, saved admin lead, owner notification and requester receipt. With analytics consent granted, confirm `generate_lead` arrives in GA4. Check mobile too. Keep test records separate from real prospects.
2. **Check discovery.** Submit the current sitemap in Search Console. Inspect the website, Westland, Detroit, software, SEO and marketing destinations. Review any exclusion reason before changing the page. Check Google's selected canonical for contact URLs with service parameters.
3. **Verify local presence.** Check for an existing Google Business Profile and confirm ownership and eligibility before creating a duplicate. Use the real Westland base and actual meeting hours/service areas. If eligible as a service-area business, follow Google's address-display rules. Remote-only work does not establish eligibility.
4. **Get permission for proof.** Ask existing clients for an honest testimonial and permission to show relevant work. Expand one website case study with the original problem, your contribution, screenshots, delivered scope and client-confirmed outcomes. Use measured outcomes only when records support them.
5. **Publish one useful project post.** Use the draft below with the appropriate case-study link. Explain what you built and who it helps.
6. **Start five relevant conversations.** Begin with existing contacts and local referral partners. Personalize each message using a real observation. Avoid bulk messages and unsolicited repeated follow-ups. Nothing has been sent automatically.

## Weekly review

Track these privately; never put prospect names or contact details in this public repository:

| Date / channel | Qualified conversations | Confirmed enquiries | Proposals | Wins | Main objection / next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Record in a private spreadsheet or the existing lead admin | | | | | |

Use a fixed reporting window for Search Console and GA4. Compare matched periods once enough data exists; label test activity and account for consent-denied visitors. Campaign labels use lowercase, non-personal values; do not put client names, emails or phone numbers in UTM parameters.

- Few relevant impressions: inspect indexing, offer coverage, legitimate local presence and authority.
- Impressions without clicks: review query intent, page title and the offer shown in search.
- Qualified visits without enquiries: review proof, pricing clarity, CTA and form completion.
- Enquiries without proposals/wins: review fit, scope, response time and recurring objections.

A link click is not a confirmed enquiry. A proposal or sale is a separate outcome from an analytics event. Do not start paid advertising until delivery and measurement are verified; any later test needs an explicit budget and review point.

## Search and AI expectations

The published service pages, accurate identity, crawlable structure, useful answers and original project evidence improve the foundation for discovery and buyer decisions. Higher ranks, AI citations and leads remain outcomes to measure, not promises.

Google's AI search features use the normal SEO foundation. Google does not use `llms.txt` as a ranking signal. Other assistants may behave differently; this work does not prove inclusion in them. Relevant first-hand project evidence is a better next investment than many near-identical keyword/city pages.
